UGLC-SC / Faculties / FACSEG

Economics & Management

One of UGLC-SC's four faculties.

The Faculty of Economics and Management (FACSEG) descends from the Institut Polytechnique de Conakry, founded in 1964, which gave rise to the École Supérieure d'Administration and, in 1990, to today's faculty. Based on the Sonfonia campus since October 2005, it trains economists and managers able to analyze and steer contemporary economic challenges.

The faculty has long benefited from the technical and financial support of UNDP Guinea, and today maintains partnerships with companies to facilitate internships and graduate employment.

Admission follows the Baccalaureate, preferably from the Mathematical Sciences track (a strong level in mathematics and statistics is essential). Two routes exist: public assignment for State scholarship holders, or the fee-paying track based on an application file, sometimes followed by a motivation interview.
