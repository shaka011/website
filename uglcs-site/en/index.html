Our identity

A public university rooted in academic rigor

Founded by Decree N°2005/104/MESRS/CAB/DRH of January 26, 2005 and named in honor of General Lansana Conté, UGLC-SC was born from the relocation of part of the then-overcrowded Université Gamal Abdel Nasser de Conakry. Since then it has trained thousands of students every year across four faculties, backed by active doctoral schools and research laboratories.

Academic pathway

From bachelor’s to doctorate, an LMD continuum

The Licence-Master-Doctorate system structures all our programs, from entry after the Baccalaureate to advanced research.

L

Licence

3 years after the Baccalaureate, in one of our 15 faculties and departments.

M

Master

2 years of advanced specialisation — 6 programs, from Private Law to Health Economics.

D

Doctorat

180 credits within our doctoral schools, for future researchers and experts.
