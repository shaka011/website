UGLC-SC / Faculties / FACSJP

Law & Political Science

One of UGLC-SC's four faculties.

The Faculty of Law and Political Science (FACSJP) is the direct descendant of the École Supérieure d'Administration, founded in 1963, which became the Faculty of Administrative and Legal Sciences in 1980, then the Faculty of Law, Economics and Management in 1990. It now prepares students for careers in the judiciary, the bar, public administration and diplomacy.
