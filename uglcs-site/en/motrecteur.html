The Rector's word

Pr. Daniel Lamah, Rector of UGLC-SC

Dear students, dear colleagues,

May this new year be one of great achievement for our institution and for our families. Each new academic year is an occasion for me to restate our direction: to keep improving the quality of our academic and scientific output, because only real work truly counts.

UGLC-SC now ranks among the largest higher-education institutions in Guinea. With that standing comes responsibility: we must keep striving for an honorable place in the sub-region, alongside the best West African universities.

Revitalized research

Working with the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, we have revitalized our doctoral schools and research laboratories, aiming for stronger scientific output and better-qualified faculty. Our motto, "UGLC-SC, a university of reference," is not a slogan — it is a daily effort we carry out together, staff and students alike.

Continued openness to the world

We keep building exchanges with recognized institutions across Africa, Europe and beyond. Our university was invited to an international rectors' conference held in Seoul, and won, alongside five other institutions, a project aimed at improving the governance of Guinean higher-education institutions.

Building together, in peace

Capacity-building sessions continue, led by respected instructors. Reliable internet access across the whole campus is being prepared, as is the renewal of several leadership roles within the Rectorate, the doctoral schools and our laboratories. I say it with conviction: peace is the prerequisite for any quality work. Let us cultivate it, every day, on our campus.

Pr. Daniel Lamah
Rector of UGLC-SC

A public higher-education institution, UGLC-SC has trained the leaders, researchers and citizens who build tomorrow's Guinea since 2005, in Sonfonia, Conakry.
