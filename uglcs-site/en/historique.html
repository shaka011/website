Our history

From the 2005 relocation to a university of reference in Guinea.

Since 2005

A trajectory serving Guinean higher education

Twenty years after its creation, UGLC-SC has become one of the country's largest public institutions.

2005

Founded by Decree N°2005/104/MESRS/CAB/DRH of January 26, as the University of Human, Legal and Economic Sciences of Sonfonia-Conakry, created by relocating part of the Université Gamal Abdel Nasser (UGANC), then built for 1,500 seats but hosting roughly 16,000 students.

2006

Renamed in honor of General Lansana Conté on January 14. The university becomes a full member of the Agence Universitaire de la Francophonie (AUF).

2008

Restructuring by decrees of October 18: the École Supérieure du Tourisme et de l'Hôtellerie becomes autonomous, and the former Faculty of Arts and Human Sciences splits into the Faculty of Arts and Language Sciences (FACLSL) and the Faculty of Social Sciences (FACSS).

2010

Creation of the Doctoral School of Humanities and Social Sciences (EDSHS) by decree of September 28 — the first doctoral students are welcomed in March 2012.

2013–2015

Signing of several major international agreements: Université Cheikh Anta Diop de Dakar, Stendhal Grenoble, Hassan II Mohammedia-Casablanca and the Interdisciplinary Research Institute of the University of São Paulo. Opening of the LASAG and LARSODED research labs.

2016

Sixteen master's programs become operational across the four faculties, and the university decentralizes its financial subvention to faculties and departments for greater management autonomy.

2020

The EDSHS's first thesis defense under international co-supervision (INALCO Paris), on social change among the Fula of Fuuta-Jaloo.

2026

UGLC-SC continues its digital modernization and launches its new bilingual institutional website, with online applications.

18,000+ students enrolled as early as 2016, with roughly 9,000 on campus every day.
